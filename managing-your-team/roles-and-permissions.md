# Roles & Permissions

Every team member has a **role** that determines what they can do, and a **status** that reflects where they are in the account lifecycle. This page explains both.

> **Administrators only.** Team Management is available to users with the **Admin** role.

## Roles

A user's role is chosen when you [add them](add-a-user.md).

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **User** | Standard access — configure data sources and panel filters, create and run analyses, and review and download results. |
| **Admin** | Full access — everything a User can do, plus **Team Management**: add users, edit accounts, reset passwords, and deactivate or reactivate accounts. |

## Account statuses

The **Status** column in the team table shows each account's current state.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending Login** | The account has a temporary password (just created, or recently [reset](reset-a-password.md)) and the user hasn't signed in yet. |
| **Active** | The user has signed in and the account is in normal use. |
| **Deactivated** | The account is disabled — the user can't sign in, and no changes can be made until it's [reactivated](manage-a-user.md#reactivate-an-account). |
