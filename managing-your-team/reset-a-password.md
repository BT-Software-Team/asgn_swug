# Reset a Password

If a user forgets their password or loses the temporary one you shared, reset it to a new temporary password. They'll use it to sign in and can then set their own.

> **Administrators only.** Team Management is available to users with the **Admin** role.

## Reset the password

1. Click your avatar in the top right, then click **Team Management**. Click the user's row to open their details.

2. Click the **⋯** (Account actions) menu and choose **Reset Password**.

3. On **Set Temporary Password**, a strong password is filled in for you. Keep it, edit it, or click **Generate** for a new one, then click **Continue**.

4. On **New Password Generated**, click the copy icon and share the password with the user securely.

   > **Important:** The password is shown only on this screen and can't be viewed again after you close the window.

5. Click **Done**.

The user's status changes to **Pending Login** until they sign in with the new temporary password.

> **Note:** Reset Password isn't available for a **Deactivated** account. [Reactivate](manage-a-user.md#reactivate-an-account) it first.
