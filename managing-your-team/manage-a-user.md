# Manage a User

Open any team member to update their profile details or, when someone leaves or no longer needs access, deactivate their account (and reactivate it later if needed).

> **Administrators only.** Team Management is available to users with the **Admin** role.

## Open a user's details

1. Click your avatar in the top right, then click **Team Management**.
2. Click the user's row in the team table. Their details open in a drawer on the right.

## Edit profile details

You can update a user's **First name**, **Last name**, and **Username** (3–30 characters, must be unique).

1. Change the value in any field. A **save** icon appears at the end of the field once you've made a change.
2. Click the **save** icon (or press **Enter**) to save that field. Each field saves on its own.

The panel also shows read-only information: **Created on**, **Activated on**, and **Status**.

## Deactivate an account

Deactivating blocks the user from signing in and prevents any further changes to their account until it's reactivated.

1. Open the user, then click the **⋯** (Account actions) menu.
2. Click **Deactivate Account**.
3. In the **Deactivate account?** confirmation, click **Deactivate**.

The user's status changes to **Deactivated**. You can't deactivate your own account.

## Reactivate an account

1. Open a user whose status is **Deactivated** and click the **⋯** menu.
2. Click **Reactivate Account**.

The user's status returns to **Active** and they can sign in again.
