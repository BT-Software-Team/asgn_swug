# Add a User

Adding a team member creates their account and sets a temporary password you share with them. They use it to sign in the first time, then choose their own password.

> **Administrators only.** Team Management is available to users with the **Admin** role.

## Open Team Management

1. Click your avatar in the top right, then click **Team Management**.

   The team table lists everyone on your team with their **Name**, **Date Created**, **Role**, and **Status**. Use the toolbar's **search** box to find a user by username, or click **Filter** to narrow the table by **Role** or **Status**.

## Add the user

1. Click **Add Team Member**.

2. In the **Add User** dialog, fill in the profile fields:

   | Field | Notes |
   |-------|-------|
   | **First** | The user's first name |
   | **Last** | The user's last name |
   | **Username** | 3–30 characters; must be unique |

3. Under **Role**, choose **User** (standard access) or **Admin** (full access, including team management). See [Roles & Permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

4. Click **Next**.

5. On **Set Temporary Password**, type a password or click **Generate** to create a strong one automatically. Then click **Continue** — this creates the account.

6. On **New Password Generated**, click the copy icon to copy the password, and share it with the user securely.

   > **Important:** For security, the password is shown only on this screen — it can't be viewed again after you close the window. If it's lost, [reset the password](reset-a-password.md).

7. Click **Done**.

The new user appears in the team table with the status **Pending Login** until they sign in for the first time.
