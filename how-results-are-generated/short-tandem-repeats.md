# Short Tandem Repeats

Repetitive DNA sequences (short motifs repeated in tandem) sized by read-based analysis.

**Applicable genes:** CFTR, FMR1

## FMR1 CGG/AGG

FMR1 CGG repeat expansions are detected and sized by read-based analysis. AGG interrupts within CGG tracts are also detected and reported.
